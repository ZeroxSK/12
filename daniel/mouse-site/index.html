What eDPI do pros use?

CS2 pros cluster around 700–900 eDPI (e.g. 400 DPI × 2.0 sens). Valorant pros sit around 200–350 eDPI (e.g. 800 DPI × 0.35). Start there, adjust ±20% to taste.

eDPI = DPI × in-game sens
400 × 2.0 = 800 eDPI (CS2)
800 × 0.35 = 280 eDPI (VAL)

FAQ

Wired or wireless for comp?

Wireless. Modern 2.4GHz + 4K/8K polling matches wired latency. Every mouse here is stage-proven wireless.

Ultralight vs 60g+?

Fingertip aimers go 49–55g (Pulsar, Viper V3 Pro). Palm/claw hybrid players often prefer 60–65g stability (Superlight 2, EC-CW).

What shape for claw grip?

Smaller symmetrical mice: G Pro X Superlight 2, Viper V3 Pro, Pulsar X2V2. Palm grip: DeathAdder V3 Pro, EC2-CW, G703-class ergos.
